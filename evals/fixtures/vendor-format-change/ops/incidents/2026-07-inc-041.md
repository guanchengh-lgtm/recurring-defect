# INC-041 — billing reconciliation produced zeros

2026-07-28. Duration ~6h. Detected by a finance analyst, not by us.

## What happened

Our payments vendor changed the `settled_at` field in their `/v2/settlements` response from
ISO-8601 (`"2026-07-28T04:00:00Z"`) to epoch milliseconds (`1785211200000`). No changelog
entry, no version bump, no deprecation notice.

`src/vendor_client.py` parses `settled_at` inside a try/except that returns None on failure.
Every settlement therefore parsed as None, fell outside the reconciliation window, and the
report showed zero settled volume for six hours.

## Contributing factors

- The parse failure was swallowed and logged at DEBUG.
- We have no assertion anywhere about the shape of the vendor's response.

## Prior occurrences

None. First time this vendor has changed a field format without notice in three years of use.
